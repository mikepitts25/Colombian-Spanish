# Colombian Spanish: competition and positioning

Research refreshed **2026-10-16**. This replaces the unsourced March 2025 comparison. Official product/help pages establish advertised capabilities, not independently measured quality, availability in every account or learning efficacy. No paid account was purchased and no competitor runtime was tested. Prices, market-size estimates, invented review sentiment and unsupported exclusivity claims have been removed. [MASTER_PLAN.md](MASTER_PLAN.md) is the work checklist.

The opportunity is a focused preparation-and-practice loop for everyday situations in Colombia. That is a product hypothesis, not an empty market: Colombian teachers, local materials, general courses, media tools and exchanges already compete for the learner's time. Adding an AI chat alone would not differentiate this app.

These are representative alternatives, not a complete market census. Absence of a feature from a public page is not evidence that a competitor lacks it. Regional depth, accessibility, offline behavior and prices require account/platform-specific validation before public comparative claims. The Duolingo investor PDF was indexed by search but a direct fetch timed out; the linked product article independently supports the core conversation capability, while exact tier access remains unverified in-app.

## What this repository can substantiate now

As of `57c8bc8`, data validation finds **47 decks, 1,392 cards and 1,392 bundled generated pronunciation mappings**. These are development-library counts, not the accepted release catalogue. The editorial ledger has no currently accepted cards. Region tags include inference; they do not certify native regional usage.

- Implemented: local flashcard scheduling, bounded preference-aware review suggestions, custom cards/favorites, recognition quizzes, separate learning observations, bilingual interface, file/clipboard backups with recovery and saved speed/opt-in reveal speech.
- Development previews: one authored text mission and three generated listening clips, typed/cloze checks, hints/corrections, exact restart/resume and optional difficult-phrase self-check. They remain gated editorial drafts, not released conversation courses.
- Verified: automated behavior and named Expo Go iPhone/iPad simulations recorded in the master plan. CI generates native projects and production JavaScript bundles; it does not establish signed native/device acceptance.
- Still missing: accepted human dialogue/voice metadata, assessed speaking, complete applied daily plans, light theme/large-text polish, validated rewards/story loop, Android native runtime acceptance and learner outcome evidence. Physical audio/accessibility and signed offline cold launch remain open.

Keep free-form hosted conversation, social features and paid services conditional. They need a concrete advantage, operating budget and owner decisions; the first useful mission need not wait for them. Reliability and a small accepted library take priority over multiplying draft cards or decorative rewards.

## Public claim boundaries

Use this development positioning: **“Spanish practice for everyday situations in Colombia, with manageable review and progress you can back up.”** It describes the intended focus; publish release copy only after the candidate and accepted library support it.

Avoid exclusivity (“only”), accent rankings (“clearest”), blanket authenticity/native recording claims, guaranteed fluency, algorithm-based efficacy promises, unsupported competitor weaknesses, fabricated testimonials and arbitrary benchmark targets. Generated audio must be labeled; self-ratings/recognition are not measured unaided mastery. Do not advertise gated previews, planned sync, unimplemented payments or an unverified offline release as shipped features.
