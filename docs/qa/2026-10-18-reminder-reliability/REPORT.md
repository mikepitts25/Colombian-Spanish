# Reminder reliability and truthful settings

Base `1547f29`. Implements the existing B12.5 reminder-settings audit and a bounded portion of B08.2-B/B08.5-B. Plan: [reminder reliability](../../superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-reminder-reliability.md).

## Failures and correction

The service previously serialized durable storage only. Native cancellation/scheduling could overlap a launch refresh and later disable, leaving stale reminders after the newer saved off intent. The regression uses a held first native schedule and a real storage save; the original implementation leaves active requests. The service now orders whole operations, including refresh reads and save writes, and recovers its queue after failure. Input preference objects are copied at dispatch. The scheduler returns scheduled/off/denied/error outcomes. Durable-write errors still reject before touching native requests; scheduling failure retains saved choices and attempts to clear partial requests. Cleanup can also fail: that remains an error, never a success claim.

Known stored boolean fields are normalized independently; unknown/malformed fields do not override defaults. Legacy streak/achievement booleans remain portable and survive supported edits. No enabled reminder choices clears requests without requesting permission. A denied permission with canAskAgain false is not re-prompted. Launch refresh still never prompts. Fixed-date tests establish seven local calendar mornings before 08:00 and six at/after 08:00; evening remains a daily 21:00 request. This does not establish DST or live-zone behavior.

The old screen allowed edits before loading, ignored rejected saves, offered nonfunctional streak/achievement switches and a weekday chevron even though reminders are daily. It now locks loading/pending controls, guards duplicate intents synchronously, rolls back a failed durable save, distinguishes saved-but-denied/scheduling-error outcomes, and offers explicit retry. Opening the screen does not schedule or prompt. Back works during loading/errors, and is locked during a save. Subordinate choices stay stored and disabled when the master is off. Unsupported alert switches/weekday selector were removed; explanatory copy and Settings subtitle describe actual capabilities. New custom times, weekday selection, streak alerts and achievement alerts were not implemented.

`es-max-before.png` reproduces live text-size clipping; `es-default-before.png` records the previous misleading controls. Keying native content by fontScale preserves owner state/refs while rebuilding measurements. Header/content scroll together, controls stack with large text, Back has a 48-point minimum and native switches have named checked/disabled semantics, 48-point containing space and expanded hit slop. Font changes may reset focus and scroll position. Tests establish draft/pending behavior, not native geometry.
