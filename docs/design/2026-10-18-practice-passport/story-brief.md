# First Morning — draft character and scene brief

Status: AI editorial preparation for B10.2, not accepted teaching content or production script. The existing [First Morning draft](../../../src/missions/firstMorning.ts) and [recording brief](../../editorial/FIRST_MORNING_RECORDING_BRIEF.md) remain authoritative for what is currently authored; their acceptance and rights gates are unchanged.

## Purpose and cast

Make the same small practical task feel like a recognizable encounter, without adding a second curriculum, another mission pack or unfamiliar language merely for a joke. The learner handles the actual greeting, request, unavailable item, alternative and thanks. Reading/typing in the present draft must not be described as a live spoken conversation.

Two provisional fictional adults, open to editor revision:

- **Ana — café staff.** Friendly and busy, with useful concise responses. She is a person with a job, not a dialect exemplar or a caricature of Colombian warmth. She can acknowledge the learner’s completed request without asserting they now speak fluently.
- **Nico — another learner/neighbor.** A companion who can make the scene less intimidating. He does not impersonate a native authority, grade pronunciation or give the assessed answer in automatic scene text. Help stays behind the existing explicit hint/model actions and retains assistance evidence.

Names, roles, pronouns, relationship and setting need product/language review. No real person, face, voice, performer attribution, regional accent, age data or learner profile is collected or implied. Reuse approved scene styling initially; illustrated portraits, recordings and additional character memory are outside this first proposal.

## Five beats within one existing mission

| Narrative beat    | Proposed story function                                                                                      | Learning and evidence boundary                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Welcome/greeting  | The learner arrives; Ana acknowledges their arrival, and Nico leaves the greeting to them.                   | Context establishes who/where without revealing the required greeting before the attempt. Optional help is labeled and recorded.                                 |
| Request           | The learner makes a clear, polite request. Nico handles the tray while the learner handles the order.        | A small cooperative moment supplies personality without adding a score, timer or assumed cultural custom. Exact accepted variants stay editorial decisions.      |
| Unavailable item  | Ana explains that the requested item is unavailable and offers a relevant alternative.                       | The obstacle is ordinary and recoverable. Recognize/produce the declared task outcome; incorrect feedback is not automatically a qualifying successful response. |
| Alternative       | The learner adapts the request. Optional help is always available.                                           | Adaptation supplies the practical win. No penalty for asking for help; a supported qualifying response is labeled supported.                                     |
| Thanks/completion | The learner closes the exchange; the scene acknowledges the outcome and, if eligible, offers the café stamp. | Commit first, then show the earned result. A merely reviewed or incomplete outcome keeps its learning record and receives a clear optional practice handoff.     |

The unavailable-item and alternative beats both fit the current `unavailable` choice node, whose accepted alternative proceeds to thanks. The proposal adds no separate alternative production step. These are five narrative beats, not five separate existing assessed nodes or newly approved dialogue lines. Any added line, changed answer, hint, branch or model requires a new content review/version/fingerprint under the current release workflow. Do not silently alter accepted transcripts or reuse a performer’s voice for words they did not record and authorize.

## Tone and review questions for the editor

Aim for a warm, lightly playful encounter in which the learner contributes something useful. Keep any humor understandable to a beginner and optional; no accent imitation, regional catchphrase quota, appearance joke, tourist-versus-local hierarchy or embarrassing public correction. A misunderstanding can be about the order and repaired politely, rather than making the learner the joke.

The editor should check who says each line, register/pronouns, accepted alternatives, realistic service interaction, regional claims and whether model text leaks an answer before assessment. Review the English framing too. Confirm that the proposed characters fit the setting without implying every café or Colombian person behaves the same way. Preserve a straightforward non-story path through the task when the learner wants efficient practice.

## AI handoff and human acceptance

AI can turn this brief into a bounded draft after the product direction is selected, attach every line to a node/purpose, prepare variant/correction tests, and apply actual editorial decisions. The owner/editor accepts the final fictional representation and exact language. Human performances/rights are optional later work under B03/B06; no speaker recruitment or recordings are part of this package.

The pilot should observe whether people understand the task and support labels, feel comfortable using hints, want another attempt, and show the independently measured language outcomes. A stamp or favorable reaction alone does not establish learning effectiveness.
