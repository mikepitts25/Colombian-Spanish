# Colombian Spanish · Christmas release assessment

**Decision as of November 24, 2026: hold public submission.** Build the launch around a small promise a learner can demonstrate: “Order breakfast at Ana’s café today. Come back and ask for something different tomorrow.” The illustrated café, short reviews, eight authored visits and practice passport give this app a reason to exist alongside a general Spanish course. The first two visits and their source phrases are the most valuable content to clear first. A December launch depends on what the editor, device testers and store can actually accept. On a fresh iPhone 17 simulator running iOS 27.2, I built the current source in Release configuration, chose Travel and Medellín through ordinary onboarding, revealed the first word, opened Home and used its recommended practice button. ## Three journeys to design around

**The first five minutes.** An English-speaking beginner currently gets a Spanish setup questionnaire, chooses a reason and region, sets a daily card count and enters Study directly. The illustrated host and the experience promised by the artwork can be missed entirely. Several reason/region choices point to paid decks and silently fall back to Greetings on a free install. Offer English / Español immediately, an optional short setup, and one free, comprehensible task. Let the learner hear or read the model, produce or recognize a reply, see what they practiced and leave with a next step. Preserve a quick route for confident learners who came for flashcards.

**Tomorrow and after an absence.** Keep the existing bounded review budget, local-day handling, saved seat and exact resume. A learner who finished breakfast should see a specific invitation such as “Back at Ana’s café · ask for water.” For the third visit, bring back a word the learner used at breakfast as a short recall cue before the next outing, using the existing review schedule and checked content. A learner midway through review should see “Continue your review” first. A returning learner with a backlog gets a short catch-up with a visible end. Only tease a visit that is actually eligible. Provide a fallback such as five due words, a saved phrase or a different free topic. Permit continuing today. Treat a streak as history and a stamp as a remembered encounter. Never imply a calendar promise the scheduler cannot keep.

**When someone might pay.** Let a traveler complete meaningful free practice and then deliberately explore another collection. Show one accepted phrase and the situation where they could use it before asking for money. Choose the otherwise confusing route from discovery → sample → localized one-time price → selected collection, with a visible route back to free learning. A paid sale must buy a clearly named, substantial library in the shipping binary.

## Visual and interaction direction

Keep the time-of-day café scene and its warm serif headline as the signature. Carry its spacing, typography hierarchy, icon language and button emphasis into onboarding, a practice card and the result screen before applying them to utilities. Keep learning text on a quiet, opaque reading surface. Limit expressive illustration to entry and reward moments. Give the flashcard phrase more space than its filters. Keep directions, examples and register readable in both languages. Preserve Colombian Spanish in the learning content while translating app chrome and stock deck names consistently. Existing `getDeckDisplayName` maps many built-in decks to English regardless of interface language. Preserve custom names verbatim.

Home currently changes the persistent tab bar’s shape, icon size, casing and selected styling when you switch tabs. Use one stable four-tab geometry, label and selected marker throughout. Derive tab colors from the surface for contrast. Reserve a solid accent for the next action, use a quiet Back affordance, outlined secondary actions and explicit destructive styling. Sound and motion are optional. Retain existing theme preferences. Test the chosen pattern in Classic, Barrio light and Barrio dark before expanding it.

## Commercial and retention scorecard

- **Keep the owner’s one-time US $19.99 target.** Regional depth, more situations, usable examples and pronunciation are the sellable value. Avoid a subscription, ads or a word limit for this release. Make the ten intended free collections, daily review, own cards, favorites, backups and core tools valuable in their own right. A materially reduced catalogue needs a revised, owner-approved offer. Published prices must come from the store.
- **Test the value before tuning price.** Ask five target learners to use a free visit, sample a premium collection and explain what they would own, how long and why it would help. Record stated willingness separately from purchases. After release, use store-reported units/proceeds/refunds and a matched cohort denominator where available. Calculate net contribution using actual proceeds less attributable editorial, audio and support costs. A low conversion rate can reflect buried samples or weak content as well as price.
- **Measure the return loop.** For a consented small pilot, record first useful response, first voluntary return on the next local day and any practice during days 6–8. Report numerator/eligible cohort size, voluntary vs reminder-prompted, median time to first response, task difficulty and one delayed unaided recall cue. Exclude staff and duplicate reinstalls when identifiable. No new tracking SDK is needed for this pilot. Store analytics alone cannot tie a named person’s first reply to a particular reminder.
- **Proposed decision criteria.** Four of five beginners find the first task and give a useful response in two minutes without coaching, including at least one English-first beginner. Four of five can identify their next task and accurately explain the price and scope after sampling. One blocking comprehension or accessibility failure on the only path merits a fix. Use day-one/week-one counts and participants’ explanations as directional signals. Never sell a five-person result as proven retention.
- **Seasonal scope.** If the core clears gates early, an editor can review an optional existing-visit introduction for travelers visiting family in December. Give it an evergreen fallback after the holidays. An Advent calendar, new currency, leaderboard, recording service or holiday-specific content pipeline would add work without resolving a current launch blocker.
