Colombian Spanish

Colombian Spanish is a vocabulary and phrase-practice app. It does not require a learner account, include advertising or purchases, or provide application cloud sync. Its core learning records are stored on your device.

  • Local learning data: Decks, custom cards and edits, favorites and flags, review schedules, daily progress, streaks, saved study sessions, quiz results, recent learning observations and preferences are stored locally. Existing optional practice checkpoints may also be retained and backed up. This storage is not an app-managed encrypted vault; device protections and operating-system backup settings apply.
  • Backups and sharing: You can explicitly export learning data to the clipboard or a JSON file and select a share or Files destination. Backups are not encrypted. Clipboard sync, recipients and external file providers may transmit or retain these copies under their own settings. Import reads the text or document you select and offers validation and confirmation before changes. Exported cache copies can remain after sharing; the app attempts to remove its own exports at least 24 hours old on later exports, without guaranteeing a deletion deadline.
  • Pronunciation and permissions: Bundled pronunciation uses generated audio. Ordinary-card playback can fall back to the device speech service, whose installed voices and network behavior depend on your operating system. The editorial-review TestFlight build does not request microphone access or upload voice recordings. It does not request location, camera, contacts or photo-library access for study. Local reminder notifications require your permission; the app does not operate a remote push-notification service.
  • Updates and diagnostics: The app is configured to use Expo for compatible application updates. Update requests can disclose connection metadata such as an IP address and technical build information to the service. The editorial-review TestFlight build does not enable the app's optional Sentry error reporting. Other explicitly configured builds may send filtered JavaScript error and build metadata to Sentry; the filter removes application messages, learning content, user/request details, breadcrumbs and source text. These providers apply their own privacy and retention practices.
  • TestFlight: Apple collects beta installation, usage, crash and feedback information and shares relevant information with us. Depending on the invitation and feedback, this can include tester identity, device/build details, comments and screenshots. We use this information to investigate problems and improve the beta; we do not share it with third parties. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.
  • Retention and deletion: Local learning records remain until you or the operating system remove or replace them. Recent observation history is limited to 5,000 entries and quiz history to ten rounds. Removing the app removes its local storage subject to device backup/restore settings. Separately delete exported files, shared copies, clipboard contents and device backups where desired. We do not hold an application-hosted learner account to delete. Beta feedback and support records are retained as reasonably necessary to investigate and resolve issues, support the beta and meet applicable obligations; Apple's retention is described in its TestFlight notice.
