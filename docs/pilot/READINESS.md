Check the actual named candidate's configuration rather than assuming that a development route or microphone permission is enabled in a release. No participant should be guided to unpublished draft content as though it were accepted training material.

The existing 1,392 generated pronunciation clips are bundled. Optional downloadable packs are proposed in B06.4 and are not implemented or published by this document. Accepted human recordings/rights and a chosen publication source remain separate.

## Reading app data alongside observations

`src/storage/learningEventSchema.ts` defines retained attempt records; `src/storage/missions.ts` stores authored run progression. Use a consented local export only when the owner has selected collection, access, retention and deletion arrangements. Do not commit a participant export, voice clip, coded observation or contact mapping into this repository.

- Keep `self-rated` and `answer-checked` evidence separate. Flashcard quality is a learner rating; checked text is evaluated against authored variants, not a holistic language judgment.
- Generated listening observations identify media/content version and record `playback-started`. That field does not establish audibility or comprehension of an authentic new speaker. Keep transcript/model assistance distinct from replay.
- `assistance: model-answer` is explicit model assistance. `hintsUsed: null` and absent historical assistance fields remain unknown. Do not infer independence from a missing field or convert every retry into an independent success.
- Mission run `completed` means the authored engine reached its completion node. Read its assisted/model-answer/hint state too. It does not prove pre/post improvement, delayed recall or transfer to a new situation. Completion and repeated checked answers are separate denominators.
- App `durationMs` represents the relevant instrumented activity interval. Keep it separate from facilitator time-to-useful-outcome, loading time and scheduled follow-up delay. Missing durations remain missing.
- The event history retains at most 5,000 observations and deduplicates retained stable IDs. A reinstall, older backup or dropped history can reduce coverage. Do not assume lifetime totals or invent absent attempts.
- Voice self-checks are unscored and are not speaking assessment events. Never derive a speaking score from a local recording's existence or from an empty speaking summary.

Manual [observations.csv](observations.csv) remains necessary for communicative subgoals, facilitator interventions, authentic novel-speaker comprehension, actual follow-up elapsed hours, intervening study and transfer. A source-code or simulator result is engineering evidence, not a row of participant data.

## Before recruitment

| Owner                             | Concrete deliverable required                                                                                               | Engineering assistance available                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Product owner                     | Named pilot candidate and scope; accepted audience, feasibility criteria, data lifecycle and contact/deletion route         | Assemble build/config/QA inventory and facilitator flow for that candidate                  |
| Language editor / recording owner | Accepted exact A/B/C/D/E prompts, alternatives, applicability, scoring examples, equivalence and novel-speaker audio/rights | Export versioned drafts and integrate attributed corrections and accepted files             |
| Authorized account owner          | Signed distribution and installation access appropriate to the selected pilot                                               | Prepare build/metadata and verify supported tooling without accepting owner-only agreements |
| Facilitator and learners          | Voluntary participation, separate optional-recording consent, actual sessions and delayed checks                            | Prepare worksheets and analyze only the collected, authorized evidence                      |
| Device tester                     | Actual supported hardware audio, permissions, interruptions and candidate checks                                            | Automate available simulator checks and make hardware steps reproducible                    |

The owner must choose a candidate before its release/device matrix can be closed. The previous unsigned SDK57 iOS QA client and automated tests are useful evidence; they are not that signed candidate. [Master plan](../../MASTER_PLAN.md), [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) and [task bank](TASK_BANK.md) retain the open gates. Passing an internal draft flow does not authorize recruitment, recording, publication or claims of effectiveness.
