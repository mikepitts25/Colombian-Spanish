## Outcome and roles

A beginner/basic adult greets café staff in the morning, requests coffee, understands that it is unavailable, chooses the offered water and thanks the staff. This is a small practice scenario, not a proficiency or dialect test. Speaker A is the visitor; speaker B is the café staff member. Medellín is the setting. Ask speakers how they identify their own regional background; do not claim that one performance represents all people from Medellín or Colombia. A later transfer check needs a different accepted speaker and wording.

Use normal courteous delivery between unfamiliar adults. The editor must assess whether the request, unavailable-item reply, address form and amount of context fit the setting and intended audience. No accent imitation, caricature or exaggerated “regional” delivery is requested. Record naturally first; avoid imposing a words-per-minute target before intelligibility is reviewed.
