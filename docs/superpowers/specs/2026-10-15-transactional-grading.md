# Recoverable flashcard grading

B02.3-B self-rated flashcard observations and B02.4-B recoverable grading subpackage. A grade commits its schedule, daily total/new-card budget, seen-word evidence, streak, self-rated recall event and optional next-session pointer through the existing durable journal. Read current persisted state inside the coordinator and serialize against history append/restore. Repeated retained attempt IDs must not apply twice; reuse with a conflicting card/session/quality must fail. Keep 5,000 retained observations; deduplication is bounded to that retained history, not a permanent ledger. Enforce the fresh-card daily budget at commit time. Unknown card calls remain no-ops.

Provider updates happen only after successful durable commit. Study keeps a stable attempt and grade through a failed save/retry, reports errors, and increments no independent counter. Difficult practice also uses guarded stable attempts and avoids advancing through a shrinking derived queue. No self-rating is presented as independently measured recall. General unrelated editor/backup operations and exact full-queue persistence remain separately tracked. No device durability claim.
