## Global Constraints

- Preserve saves, plan/session identity, limits, retry intent, topic navigation, and adventure eligibility.

- Animations stop when unfocused/backgrounded; Reduce Motion is respected live.
- No release, purchase, or account changes.

## Review Focus

- Storage errors must not freeze the time-of-day atmosphere or show stale activity.
- First/last day boundaries and foreground clock changes.

- Native driver animation cleanup and async accessibility-query races.
- Adventure load errors and eligibility remain truthful.
